About Us & Bookings

Our Philosophy

Located in the heart of **Chitwan, Nepal**, Stupidity Ink is more than just a tattoo shop—it's a sanctuary for personalized body art. We believe every piece of ink should be as unique as the person wearing it. Our focus is on **custom design**, prioritizing creativity and client collaboration to bring complex visions to life with exceptional detail and safety. We are committed to providing a clean, professional, and inspiring environment.
